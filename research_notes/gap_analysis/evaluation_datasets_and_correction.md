## Key question 1: Metrics per stage (mir_eval modules, tab-specific metrics, strumming, end-to-end usefulness)

### Takeaway
Every stage except fingering/strumming has a standard `mir_eval` function with fixed default tolerances (chord: duration-weighted accuracy over vocabularies; beat: F-measure at ±70 ms plus continuity/Cemgil/Goto/P-score/information gain; notes: P/R/F with ±50 ms onset, ±50 cent pitch, 20 %/50 ms offset; multipitch: frame-level P/R/Acc + error decomposition at ±0.5 semitone; melody: voicing + raw pitch/chroma + overall accuracy at ±50 cents). Tablature uses the TabCNN frame-level string/fret P/R/F plus Tablature Disambiguation Rate (TDR = correct string-fret / correct pitches), with 2024–2026 papers adding playability proxies (chord stretch, KL divergence of fret-string distribution, hand-span feasibility, biomechanical difficulty score) and human playability ratings; strumming is scored as per-direction onset P/R/F at a 50 ms window.

### Cited Findings

**mir_eval.chord** (source docstrings)
- Module compares estimated chord labels to a reference "usually using a mapping to a chord subalphabet"; comparison scores lie in [0,1] with **-1 meaning "ignored" (out-of-gamut)**; `weighted_accuracy(comparisons, weights)` normalises weights (interval durations) and ignores -1 scores — [mir_eval/chord.py](https://github.com/mir-evaluation/mir_eval/blob/main/mir_eval/chord.py)
- Functions and what they compare: `root` (root only); `majmin` (major, minor, no-chord only; others ignored); `majmin_inv` (adds inversions; "bass note must exist in the triad"); `thirds`/`thirds_inv` (root + third); `triads`/`triads_inv` (major, minor, aug, dim, sus through the 5th); `tetrads`/`tetrads_inv` (entire quality in closed voicing, extensions folded to one octave); `sevenths`/`sevenths_inv` (MIREX sevenths rules: only maj, maj7, 7, min, min7, N allowed); `mirex` (correct if ≥3 pitch classes shared); `overseg`, `underseg`, `seg` (= min of the two, 1.0 = perfect segmentation); `evaluate(ref_intervals, ref_labels, est_intervals, est_labels)` returns an OrderedDict with keys thirds, thirds_inv, triads, triads_inv, tetrads, tetrads_inv, root, mirex, majmin, majmin_inv, sevenths, sevenths_inv, overseg, underseg, seg — [mir_eval/chord.py](https://github.com/mir-evaluation/mir_eval/blob/main/mir_eval/chord.py)
- MIREX 2020 Audio Chord Estimation reported exactly these vocabularies (MirexRoot, MirexMajMin, MirexMajMinBass, MirexSevenths, MirexSeventhsBass, MeanSeg/UnderSeg/OverSeg) over Isophonics2009, Billboard2012/2013, JayChou29, RobbieWilliams, RWC-Popular, USPOP2002Chords and CASD-Annotator1-4; only one system (HL2) was submitted that year, scoring e.g. 77.20 MirexRoot on RobbieWilliams and 84.08 OverSeg on Isophonics2009 — [MIREX 2020 ACE results](https://music-ir.org/mirex/wiki/2020:Audio_Chord_Estimation_Results)

**mir_eval.beat**
- `trim_beats(beats, min_beat_time=5.0)` — "any beats which occur before 5s are ignored"; `f_measure(..., f_measure_threshold=0.07)` (±70 ms); `cemgil(..., cemgil_sigma=0.04)` returns (cemgil_score, cemgil_max); `goto(..., goto_threshold=0.35, goto_mu=0.2, goto_sigma=0.2)` is binary 1/0; `p_score(..., p_score_threshold=0.2)`; `continuity(..., continuity_phase_threshold=0.175, continuity_period_threshold=0.175)` returns CMLc, CMLt, AMLc, AMLt (correct/any metrical level, continuous/total); `information_gain(..., bins=41)` is K-L divergence of the beat-error histogram from uniform; `evaluate()` returns all in an OrderedDict — [mir_eval/beat.py](https://github.com/mir-evaluation/mir_eval/blob/main/mir_eval/beat.py)
- Lukoianov & Klapuri 2025 score downbeat/bar lines at a **70 ms tolerance** "matching BeatThis literature standards" — [arXiv 2510.05756](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.05756)

**mir_eval.transcription** (note events)
- Notes are (onset, offset, pitch-Hz); `precision_recall_f1_overlap(ref_intervals, ref_pitches, est_intervals, est_pitches, onset_tolerance=0.05, pitch_tolerance=50.0, offset_ratio=0.2, offset_min_tolerance=0.05, strict=False, beta=1.0)` returns (precision, recall, f_measure, avg_overlap_ratio); a match requires onset within ±50 ms, pitch within ±50 cents, and offset within max(20 % of ref duration, 50 ms); `offset_ratio=None` disables offsets; `onset_precision_recall_f1` and `offset_precision_recall_f1` isolate timing; `average_overlap_ratio` is the mean of overlap/union durations of matched pairs; `strict` toggles `<` vs `<=`; `evaluate()` returns Precision/Recall/F-measure/Average_Overlap_Ratio, the `_no_offset` variants, Onset_* and Offset_* variants — [mir_eval/transcription.py](https://github.com/mir-evaluation/mir_eval/blob/main/mir_eval/transcription.py)
- Basic Pitch's paper defines the de-facto guitar reporting trio: note F-measure F (pitch within a quarter tone, onset within 50 ms, offset within 20 % of duration), F-measure-no-offset Fno (same but ignoring offsets), and frame-level note accuracy Acc, "computed using mir_eval"; on GuitarSet NMP (Basic Pitch) scores Acc .70 / Fno .79 / F .56, and on the solo-monophonic half of GuitarSet Acc 71.7 / Fno 84.0 / F 65.0 vs the guitar-specific TENT baseline 63.2 / 76.3 / 54.6 — [arXiv 2203.09893](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.09893)
- EGDB paper: "We use the mir_eval library to compute the 'onset F1' and 'frame F1'... an onset estimate as correct when it is within a ±50ms tolerance window... We do not evaluate the offsets"; best onset F1 on real-world recordings 0.550, on DI test split 0.613 — [arXiv 2202.09907](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.09907)
- TART reports P50/R50/F50 (±50 ms onset) plus frame_ap, reg_onset_mae, reg_offset_mae, velocity_mae for its CRNN stage; GuitarSet F50 = 0.838, EGDB F50 = 0.779 — [arXiv 2510.02597](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.02597)
- GOAT reports note-level P/R/F1 for a high-resolution piano-style model fine-tuned on GOAT: DI→DI F1 0.820, AMP→GuitarSet F1 0.843 — [arXiv 2509.22655](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.22655)

**mir_eval.multipitch** (frame level)
- Inputs: time array (s) + list of per-frame frequency arrays (Hz); `resample_multipitch` nearest-neighbour resamples to a common timebase; `compute_num_true_positives(ref_freqs, est_freqs, window=0.5, chroma=False)` matches within ±0.5 semitone; `compute_accuracy` → (precision, recall, accuracy); `compute_err_score` → (e_sub, e_miss, e_fa, e_tot); `metrics()` returns a 14-tuple of raw and chroma-wrapped Precision/Recall/Accuracy/Substitution/Miss/False-Alarm/Total error; `evaluate()` wraps in an OrderedDict — [mir_eval/multipitch.py](https://github.com/mir-evaluation/mir_eval/blob/main/mir_eval/multipitch.py)

**mir_eval.melody**
- `to_cent_voicing(ref_time, ref_freq, est_time, est_freq, est_voicing=None, ref_reward=None, base_frequency=10.0, hop=None, kind="linear")`; convention: "negative frequency indicates 'Predicted as unvoiced, but if it's voiced, this is the frequency estimate'"; `voicing_measures` → (vx_recall, vx_false_alarm); `raw_pitch_accuracy`, `raw_chroma_accuracy` (ignores octave errors), `overall_accuracy` all default `cent_tolerance=50`; `evaluate()` returns Voicing Recall, Voicing False Alarm, Raw Pitch Accuracy, Raw Chroma Accuracy, Overall Accuracy — [mir_eval/melody.py](https://github.com/mir-evaluation/mir_eval/blob/main/mir_eval/melody.py)

**Source separation**
- museval computes SDR, SIR, SAR, ISR (BSS Eval v4) framewise on 1-second windows; `museval.eval_mus_track(track, estimates, output_dir=...)` writes validated JSON; MIT licence; `pip install museval` — [sigsep-mus-eval](https://github.com/sigsep/sigsep-mus-eval)
- Demucs is evaluated with museval SDR on the MUSDB18-HQ test set; htdemucs/htdemucs_ft reach "9.00 dB" (9.20 dB fine-tuned); the 6-source model adds guitar and piano stems but "the piano source exhibits significant artifacts"; MIT licence; Windows users should run inside the Anaconda console with `python.exe` — [Demucs README](https://github.com/facebookresearch/demucs/blob/main/README.md)

**Tablature-specific metrics**
- TabCNN (Wiggins & Kim, ISMIR 2019) introduced frame-level tablature metrics on GuitarSet with 6-fold cross-validation holding out one guitarist per fold (472,560 test frames, ~43 fps CQT, 192×9 input, 6×21 output classes per frame): tablature precision p_tab = correct string-fret combos / predicted combos; tablature recall r_tab = correct / ground-truth combos; tablature F-measure; and **Tablature Disambiguation Rate TDR = correct string-fret combos / correctly identified pitches** ("how frequently pitches that are correctly identified are assigned the correct tablature"). TabCNN results: multipitch P/R/F 0.900/0.764/0.826 (Deep Salience baseline 0.646 F); tablature P/R/F/TDR 0.809/0.696/0.748/0.899; paper CC BY 4.0 — [TabCNN, Zenodo 3527800](https://zenodo.org/records/3527800)
- amt-tools (Cwitkowitz) implements the same: `tdr = num_correct_tablature / (num_correct_multi_pitch + EPSILON)`, tablature precision = correct / predicted, recall = correct / ground truth, F1 via `mir_eval.util.f_measure`; note metrics delegate to `mir_eval.transcription.precision_recall_f1_overlap` and frame metrics to `mir_eval.multipitch` — [amt_tools/evaluate.py](https://github.com/cwitkowitz/amt-tools/blob/master/amt_tools/evaluate.py)
- Noise2Fret (Simionato & Bigo, 2026-08-31) reports Pitch P/R/F, Tab P/R/F, TDR, FNR, FPR; Tab F 0.845 / TDR 0.988 on GuitarSet and Tab F 0.795 / TDR 0.993 on GOAT; five auxiliary playability losses usable as metrics: Pitch-Class Distance (Jaccard on pitch-class sets), Circle-of-Fifths Distance (weighted mean CoF position), Positional Distance ("mean absolute fret distance between predicted and target fret assignments"), String Similarity (Jaccard on active string sets), Hand-Span Feasibility ("normalised excess span beyond the maximum allowable fret distance") — [arXiv 2608.30854](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30854)
- TART v1 (2025-10-02) stage metrics: technique classification accuracy 76 % over nine techniques + "other"; string/fret assignment via Fretting-Transformer uses "pitch accuracy" (correct pitch regardless of fingering) and "tab accuracy" (correct pitch AND string-fret) — 94.9 % / 42.1 % on the DadaGP acoustic subset — [arXiv 2510.02597](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.02597); TART v2 (2026-09-10) headline "81.35% audio-to-MIDI F50" and "54.08% end-to-end Tab F1" on GuitarSet/EGDB and noisy variants — [arXiv 2609.11904](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11904)
- Fretting-Transformer (Hamberger, Murgul et al., 2025-06-17): tablature accuracy = agreement of predicted string-fret with professional ground-truth tabs (0–100 %); pitch accuracy; and a biomechanical **difficulty score** (range 0–18.5 on a 24-fret guitar: moving up frets costs 0.50×Δ, moving down 0.75×Δ, locality penalty α=0.25, vertical span penalty 0.25 for ≤1-string gap / 0.50 for >1); tab accuracy with post-processing 98.41 % GuitarToday, 81.58 % DadaGP, 72.19 % Leduc, beating A*, Guitar Pro 8.1.37 and TuxGuitar 1.6.68 — [arXiv 2506.14223](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.14223)
- MIDI-to-Tab (Edwards, Riley, Sarmento, Dixon, 2024): string accuracy (agreement of note-string assignment) 73.58 % vs MuseScore 62.51 %, Guitar Pro 8 62.27 %, TuxGuitar 55.42 %; **chord stretch** (max fret distance between simultaneous notes) mean 1.84 frets vs 1.04 ground truth, 2.4 % of chords stretched >7 frets; **KL divergence of fret-string distribution** vs ground truth 0.099 (competitors 0.462–1.286); user study: 15 guitarists, 30 four-bar jazz excerpts, 5 tab versions each, 1–10 playability rating while attempting to play — system 6.04±2.67, ground truth 7.45±2.62, Guitar Pro 4.69, MuseScore 4.67, TuxGuitar 3.32 (Friedman χ²(4)=532.09, p<.001) — [arXiv 2408.05024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.05024)
- GOAT's own tablature baseline (Whisper fine-tuned on DadaGP tokens) reports token accuracy 0.68–0.69 and WER 0.515–0.547, and the authors warn high token accuracy is "misleading due to the prevalence of wait tokens" — [arXiv 2509.22655](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.22655)
- "Visual Guitar Tab Comparison" (Heyen et al., 2023) aligns tab versions bar-by-bar by inserting empty bars (Needleman–Wunsch citation) and visualises per-bar note density, fret span (lowest/highest fret or mm), technique annotations and explicit small differences; code + demo CC BY 4.0 at github.com/visvar/visual-guitar-tab-comparison; limitation: multi-sequence alignment not handled — [arXiv 2311.14726](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.14726)

**Strumming-pattern evaluation**
- Murgul, Schimper & Heizmann (2025-08-11) score strumming as precision/recall/F1 for **down strums, up strums and direction-agnostic ("any")** with a "50 ms tolerance window" following mir_eval; chord recognition as accuracy on a 24-class maj/min vocabulary; targets are regression curves (triangular function of time-to-next-strum, J=5) + 24 chord logits from a CRNN (4 conv blocks → BiGRU-256) on 229-bin log-mel at 16 kHz. Results: pickup signal F1-any 97.60 % (spectral flux 79.49 %), F1-down 87.87 % / F1-up 84.90 % (hybrid with accelerometer 90.02 / 88.66); microphone audio with synthetic+real training F1-any 92.75 %, F1-down 85.51 %, F1-up 79.02 %; chord accuracy 90.06 %. Data: 90 min real recordings from three guitarists wearing an ESP32 smartwatch accelerometer (pickup + mic), labels semi-automatic (spectral-flux onsets synced with acceleration, then manual validation); 4 h synthetic from 51 chord progressions × 36 strumming patterns rendered with DAWDreamer + virtual instruments + effects/noise augmentation — [arXiv 2508.07973](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.07973)
- Lukoianov & Klapuri (2025-10-07) transcribe rhythm-guitar **patterns** (slash notation; expert vocabulary of 924 one- or two-bar patterns, 16th-note resolution, time-signature labels, 10 empty/silence patterns; no up/down direction). Metrics: (1) **reconstructed strum F1/P/R at 50 ms** from the predicted pattern sequence; (2) **discontinuity rates** for readability — pattern changes / measures, time-signature changes / measures, and % measures whose length differs >35 % from the previous; (3) downbeat metrics at 70 ms. Results: fine-tuned MERT on the "other" stem 96.9 % strum F1; full system 94.7 % strum F1 with pattern-transition constraints cutting discontinuity from 27.8 % to 15.4 %; full-mix model 96.5 % F1 (nearly matching stems). Dataset: 410 pop/rock songs, 931 versions at four difficulty levels with isolated guitar/vocal/backing stems and expert transcriptions; only example tracks released on GitHub — [arXiv 2510.05756](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.05756)

### Inferences
- A ukulele pipeline can report, per stage, directly comparable numbers to the literature by calling: `mir_eval.chord.evaluate` (report majmin + seg), `mir_eval.beat.evaluate` (report F-measure + CMLt/AMLt), `mir_eval.transcription.evaluate` (report F, F_no_offset, Onset F), `mir_eval.multipitch.evaluate`, and a TabCNN-style string/fret P/R/F + TDR computed on a 4×(frets+1) frame matrix instead of 6×21.
- TDR is the metric that isolates the fingering stage from the pitch stage: pitch F measures the transcriber, TDR measures the string assignment given correct pitches. For re-entrant GCEA ukulele, many pitches are playable on 2–3 strings, so TDR will be the sensitive number.
- Playability proxies that need no human: chord stretch (max fret span per chord), KL divergence of fret-string histogram vs a human-tab corpus, Fretting-Transformer difficulty score, Hand-Span Feasibility. Human playability rating (1–10 on 4-bar excerpts) is the published gold standard.
- Strum direction should be scored as per-direction onset F1 at 50 ms (Murgul) and pattern-level readability as discontinuity rate (Lukoianov); both are easy to implement with `mir_eval.onset`-style matching.
- An "edit distance to human tab" metric is not standardised; the closest published analogues are GOAT's WER on DadaGP token streams and the bar-aligned diff of Heyen et al.

### Gaps
- No paper found that defines a "number of corrections needed" or token-edit-distance usefulness metric for tabs; GOAT's WER is the only token-level distance reported, and its authors call the result unreliable.
- mir_eval's rendered documentation pages (mir-evaluation.github.io/.../api/*.html) returned 404 during this session; findings were taken from the source docstrings instead.
- Lukoianov & Klapuri's full dataset is proprietary; only example tracks are public, so their discontinuity metrics cannot be reproduced on their data.

## Key question 2: Datasets (size, licence, access, annotations, full-mix vs solo)

### Takeaway
There is no public ukulele transcription dataset with timing/tab annotations; the only ukulele audio data found is a 1,960-clip isolated-chord set on Hugging Face (CC BY-NC 4.0). Guitar datasets split into solo-instrument tab sets (GuitarSet CC BY 4.0, GOAT CC BY-NC 4.0 restricted, EGDB CC BY 4.0, IDMT-SMT-Guitar CC BY-NC-ND, Guitar-TECHS CC BY 4.0, SynthTab/DadaGP by request) and full-mix sets that provide chords/beats but no guitar stems (Isophonics, Billboard, RWC, CASD) or stems but no chords (MUSDB18-HQ, MoisesDB CC BY-NC-SA, MedleyDB, Slakh CC BY 4.0).

## Key question 3: Building ukulele ground truth cheaply (human tabs, synthesis, own recordings, DTW alignment)

### Takeaway
Three complementary routes are evidenced in the literature: (a) synthesise audio from symbolic tabs with string-specific MIDI channels through SoundFonts/sample libraries (SynthTab, Murgul's DAWDreamer pipeline) — for ukulele, FreePats' CC0 SF2/SFZ and ten public-domain/WTFPL/CC-BY SF2s exist; (b) record your own short set and align the known tab to the recording semi-automatically (EGDB's per-string onset snapping; GOAT's fine-aligned MIDI; synctoolbox/librosa DTW); (c) scrape human tabs is legally constrained — Ultimate Guitar's ToS forbids reproduction beyond personal printing, and Songsterr exposes no API in its help pages.

### Cited Findings

**Human tabs as references**
- Ultimate Guitar ToS: users may only "print out text-based Tablature and/or Lyrics for your personal, non-commercial use" where licensing permits, and "will not duplicate or otherwise reproduce the Content, or any portion thereof, onto any physical medium"; probing/scanning the service and collecting information from others without consent are prohibited; contributors grant UG a non-exclusive, sublicensable licence — [UG ToS](https://www.ultimate-guitar.com/about/tos.htm)
- Songsterr help: all tabs are "contributed by users"; the tab editor opens with **E**, notes are edited by clicking, arrow-key navigation and typing digits 0–9; revisions are tracked by date under the title; "No information about API access or programmatic data retrieval is provided"; Songsterr states "we don't have many ukulele tabs and don't get many requests from ukulele players" — [Songsterr help](https://www.songsterr.com/a/wa/help); Songsterr Plus adds "Transcribe 50 complete tabs per month" (AI) and MIDI/Guitar Pro/MP3/WAV downloads — [Songsterr Plus](https://www.songsterr.com/a/wa/plus)
- The old documented Songsterr API URL (songsterr.com/a/wa/api) and the legacy JSON endpoint (/a/ra/songs.json) both returned 404 on 2026-10-01 — [songsterr.com/a/wa/api](https://www.songsterr.com/a/wa/api)
- DadaGP (26,181 GP files) and GOAT are the research-grade tab corpora; GOAT strips song/artist names for copyright protection — [DadaGP](https://github.com/dada-bots/dadaGP); [arXiv 2509.22655](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.22655)
- PyGuitarPro reads/writes GP3/GP4/GP5 (transpose, change strings), LGPL-3.0, `pip install PyGuitarPro`; GP6+/.gpx not mentioned — [PyGuitarPro](https://github.com/Perlence/PyGuitarPro); TabForge (MIT, .NET 8) uses alphaTab (MPL-2.0) to read/write .gp/.gp5/.gpx and supports ukulele tunings — [cobhc95/TabForge](https://github.com/cobhc95/TabForge)
- OpenUkulele already exports ASCII tab, GP5 and a JSON playability report from Basic Pitch output, assuming high-G re-entrant G4-C4-E4-A4, with DP fingering per measure, span ≤4–5 frets, barre feasibility checks and automatic simplification ("remove harmony → remove bass → melody only"); 120 tests; MIT; Python 3.11+ — [xin-pu/OpenUkulele](https://github.com/xin-pu/OpenUkulele)

**Synthesising ukulele audio from tabs**
- SynthTab pipeline: GuitarPro → JAMS → MIDI, "string-specific MIDI channels ensure correct string assignments during synthesis", vibrato humanisation via MIDI CC, rendered across 23 timbres — [synthtab.dev](https://synthtab.dev/)
- Murgul 2025 synthetic strumming data: sample 51 progressions × 36 patterns, render with DAWDreamer + virtual instruments, augment with effects/reverb/noise — 4 h — [arXiv 2508.07973](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.07973)
- Pedroza 2024 GuitarSetFX/GuitarProFX: re-amp with real guitar tones + effects to make 360-track synthetic sets — [arXiv 2405.14679](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.14679)
- FreePats Ukulele: CC0 1.0; SFZ (FLAC 2.3 MiB / WAV 2.8 MiB) and SF2 (1.5 MiB); two octaves C4–C6 plus body-tap (B2) and "chuck" (C3) articulations (~26 notes); Flight Fireball tenor, RØDE Podcaster; release 2026-08-11 on GitHub — [FreePats ukulele](https://freepats.zenvoid.org/GuitarFamily/ukulele.html)
- musical-artifacts.com lists 11 ukulele artifacts, including SF2s under public-domain (Sadiquecat ver., Mahalo Hano, little-scale's), WTFPL (RemyMarshal's, Makala Plucked), CC BY (Caed's) and CC BY-SA (Heiskr/Hedsound) licences — [musical-artifacts JSON](https://musical-artifacts.com/artifacts.json?q=ukulele)
- pyFluidSynth: Python bindings for FluidSynth ≥2.0; Windows needs the libfluidsynth DLL from the FluidSynth GitHub releases; offline rendering via `Synth.get_samples(len)` returning NumPy arrays; LGPL-2.1+; `pip install pyfluidsynth` — [pyfluidsynth](https://github.com/nwhitehead/pyfluidsynth)

**Recording your own set and aligning known tabs**
- EGDB's semi-automatic alignment: compute expected onsets from the tab at the recording BPM, run per-string onset detection on the DI, snap each actual onset to the nearest expected onset, derive offsets as onset + expected duration; "Only very few onset labels have to be" fixed manually — [arXiv 2202.09907](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.09907)
- GOAT provides both tab-derived MIDI and "fine-aligned MIDI notes to the performances" — [arXiv 2509.22655](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.22655)
- GuitarSet used a hexaphonic pickup plus a reference mic so per-string pitch could be tracked; EGSet12 was 12 pieces composed and played by one professional (≈6 min total) and still served as a credible real-world test set — [GuitarSet](https://guitarset.weebly.com/); [arXiv 2405.14679](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.14679)
- Murgul used an ESP32 smartwatch accelerometer to label strum direction semi-automatically (spectral-flux onsets synced with acceleration, manual validation) — [arXiv 2508.07973](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.07973)
- synctoolbox (Müller et al., JOSS 2021): MsDTW / memory-restricted MrMsDTW with chroma + onset features for audio-to-audio and audio-to-score alignment; `pip install synctoolbox`, Python 3.7+, MIT code (bundled data under separate terms); notebooks at groupmm.github.io/synctoolbox — [synctoolbox](https://github.com/meinardmueller/synctoolbox)
- `librosa.sequence.dtw(X=None, Y=None, *, C=None, metric="euclidean", step_sizes_sigma=None, weights_add=None, weights_mul=None, subseq=False, backtrack=True, global_constraints=False, band_rad=0.25, return_steps=False)` returns the accumulated cost D (D[-1,-1] = total cost) and warping path wp (L×2); `subseq=True` enables subsequence alignment; the docstring example aligns chroma features — [librosa/sequence.py](https://github.com/librosa/librosa/blob/main/librosa/sequence.py)
- Tony (QMUL) gives pYIN pitch/note transcription of monophonic recordings with "alternative pitch track selection, octave shift, and easy note split, merge and deletion", CSV export, Windows/Linux/Mac, open source — [Tony](https://www.sonicvisualiser.org/tony/)

### Inferences
- Cheapest timing ground truth: write the tab in MuseScore/Guitar Pro/TabForge (ukulele tuning), render it through FreePats CC0 SF2 with per-string MIDI channels (SynthTab method) to get exact labels, then record a human take to a click and transfer labels with synctoolbox MrMsDTW (synth render ↔ real take) followed by EGDB-style onset snapping; manual fix-up in Sonic Visualiser or Tony.
- Scraping Ultimate Guitar is explicitly contrary to its ToS; Songsterr offers no API; using human tabs as references should rely on self-authored tabs, DadaGP/GOAT (research request), or the user's own purchased GP files.
- A ukulele "EGSet12"-style set (≈12 pieces, ≈6–10 min) is consistent with published test-set sizes and is enough for per-stage regression numbers.

### Gaps
- Commercial ukulele libraries (Ample Sound, Native Instruments) could not be verified: the Ample Sound product page returned 404 and no other vendor page was fetched; strumming-engine EULA terms on rendering datasets are unknown.
- Karplus–Strong synthesis for ukulele: no source fetched; untested claim, omitted.
- No published description of how Chordify or Moises create or validate their own chord ground truth beyond CASD.

## Key question 4: Regression test harness design for an MIR pipeline

### Takeaway
The reference open-source repos test with golden outputs and tight numeric tolerances (Basic Pitch: `assert_allclose(..., atol=1e-4, rtol=0)` against saved model outputs on a fixed resource WAV, plus range and file-format checks); dataset loaders with checksum validation (mirdata) and golden-file fixtures (pytest-regressions with `--force-regen`) supply the remaining pieces; separation is benchmarked with museval JSON per track.

### Cited Findings
- Basic Pitch `tests/test_inference.py`: uses the fixture `vocadito_10.wav` in `resources` with precomputed expected outputs; asserts `np.testing.assert_allclose(expected_model_output[k], model_output[k], atol=1e-4, rtol=0)`; checks `set(model_output.keys()) == set(["note", "onset", "contour"])`, note pitch ranges (`n[2] >= lowest_supported_midi`), that MIDI/CSV/NPZ/WAV outputs exist on disk (`test_predict_with_saves`), and that onset/frame thresholds and min/max-frequency filters behave across values — [basic-pitch tests/test_inference.py](https://github.com/spotify/basic-pitch/blob/main/tests/test_inference.py)
- Basic Pitch paper evaluation protocol: datasets with train/test counts (GuitarSet 648/72 tracks, MAESTRO 1154/128, Slakh 1590/120, Phenicx –/42, Molina –/38) and metrics computed with mir_eval; results compared by paired t-test at p<0.05 — [arXiv 2203.09893](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.09893)
- Demucs evaluates with museval SDR on MUSDB18-HQ test; `demucs -n htdemucs_ft file.mp3`; MIT — [Demucs README](https://github.com/facebookresearch/demucs/blob/main/README.md)
- museval writes "validated JSON files in a standardized format" per track (SDR/SIR/SAR/ISR framewise, 1 s windows) — [sigsep-mus-eval](https://github.com/sigsep/sigsep-mus-eval)
- mirdata: dataset download to a standard location, **validation that all expected files are present**, loading annotations "in a format compatible with mir_eval", metadata parsing; loaders include GuitarSet, DALI, MAESTRO; BSD-3-Clause; `pip install mirdata` — [mirdata](https://github.com/mir-dataset-loaders/mirdata)
- pytest-regressions provides fixtures for "general data, images, files, and numeric tables", regenerates baselines with `--force-regen`; MIT — [pytest-regressions](https://github.com/ESSS/pytest-regressions)
- TabCNN's protocol — 6-fold leave-one-player-out on GuitarSet, mean ± std over folds — is the standard split for tab models — [TabCNN](https://zenodo.org/records/3527800)
- EGDB's protocol — train on DI or 3-amp renders, test on two held-out amps and on real-world recordings — is the standard timbre-shift check — [arXiv 2202.09907](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.09907)
- GOAT reports cross-dataset matrices (train DI/AMP/AMP-XL × test DI/AMP/GuitarSet) — [arXiv 2509.22655](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.22655)
- OpenUkulele ships "120 unit, integration and CLI tests" plus 26 xUnit tests for its desktop shell and emits a JSON playability report per output — [xin-pu/OpenUkulele](https://github.com/xin-pu/OpenUkulele)

### Inferences
- A pytest harness for this pipeline: (1) cache separated stems and CQTs keyed by audio hash; (2) golden-file tests per stage (chords `.lab`, beats `.txt`, notes `.csv`, tab JSON) via `pytest-regressions` `file_regression`/`data_regression`; (3) metric-threshold tests calling `mir_eval.*.evaluate` against the ukulele test set with per-metric minimums (e.g. note F_no_offset, chord majmin, beat F-measure, tab TDR) and a numeric regression (`num_regression`) to catch silent drift; (4) `assert_allclose` with `atol=1e-4` on raw model posteriorgrams for determinism, as Basic Pitch does; (5) a timbre-shift split (synth render vs real recording) mirroring EGDB.
- Leave-one-recording-out or leave-one-player-out folds (TabCNN) are more honest than random splits when the ukulele set is small.

### Gaps
- No evidence was found of MLflow or Weights & Biases usage inside the Basic Pitch, Demucs, amt-tools or TART repositories (not fetched/searchable in this session); treat "MLflow/W&B in MIR projects" as unverified.
- The Demucs repository's own unit-test directory was not inspected (README only).

## Key question 5: Human-in-the-loop correction (products, UI patterns, open tools, exporting corrections)

### Takeaway
Shipping products converge on three UI patterns: a beat/bar-aligned chord timeline with per-chord edit (Moises, Chordify*), a keyboard-driven tab editor with per-note digit entry and revision history (Songsterr, TabForge, MuseScore), and a sync-point editor that aligns notation to a recording (Soundslice). Open tools cover the research side: Sonic Visualiser (draw/edit/erase annotation layers; CSV/MIDI/SVL/RDF import-export), Tony (note split/merge/delete with alternative pitch tracks), Audacity label tracks (tab-separated start/end/label), and Label Studio (read-only `predictions` JSON that annotators correct into `annotations`, then export).

### Cited Findings
- Moises: shows "the full chord progression, synced to the audio"; guitar diagrams "adjusted for your capo position"; FAQ: "you also have the option to manually edit chords so they appear correctly moving forward"; chord-chart export is editable "right on the web page, from the title down to any line of the sheet" and exports iReal Pro / MusicXML; accuracy statement: "Most users find our chord detection is quite accurate, but it does occasionally make mistakes"; separating stems first yields "cleaner, more accurate chord detection" — [Moises chord detection](https://moises.ai/features/chord-detection/)
- Songsterr: editor toggled with **E**; click a note for its context menu; arrow keys to navigate; type 0–9 or backspace; auto-save when signed in; revision history by date link; only the latest own revision can be deleted; AI Transcribe produces a new draft version; no formal review process described — [Songsterr help](https://www.songsterr.com/a/wa/help)
- Soundslice syncpoint editor: "join together notation and real recordings"; add/move/delete/copy syncpoints, automatic syncpoints, guide notes, recording cropping, non-sequential syncpoints, multitrack stems; separate notation/tab editor and PDF/photo import — [Soundslice syncing](https://www.soundslice.com/help/en/creating/syncing/163/overview/); [Soundslice help](https://www.soundslice.com/help/)
- MuseScore 4 tablature staff: tuning via the "String Data" dialog with automatic fret adjustment, add/remove strings, max-fret setting, "Simple/Common/Full" tab styles, per-staff and global tablature style settings — [MuseScore handbook](https://handbook.musescore.org/idiomatic-notation/guitar/customizing-a-tablature-staff)
- TabForge (open source, MIT, .NET 8): keyboard-first entry (type fret numbers, arrow keys, `+`/`-` durations), >30 techniques, two voices, instrument-aware fretboard "including ukulele, mandolin", VST2/3 hosting; v0.5 pre-release — [cobhc95/TabForge](https://github.com/cobhc95/TabForge)
- Sonic Visualiser: five editable layer types (Time Instants, Time Values, Notes, Regions, Text); Draw tool click/drag, Edit tool drag/double-click, Erase tool, delete-selected; imports/exports CSV or tab-separated text, Standard MIDI (note layers), SVL XML, RDF/Turtle; Vamp plugins via Transform → Analysis — [SV reference manual](https://www.sonicvisualiser.org/doc/reference/4.0/en/)
- Tony: pYIN pitch tracks and notes for monophonic recordings; corrections by alternative pitch track selection, octave shift, note split/merge/delete; CSV export; Windows/Linux/Mac; cite Mauch et al. 2015 (TENOR) — [Tony](https://www.sonicvisualiser.org/tony/)
- Audacity label tracks: point and region labels (a point label "is just a region label with the same start and end"), drag handles, Label Editor spreadsheet; export/import tab-delimited .txt with start, end, label; **Ctrl+M** adds a label at the playback position without stopping — [Audacity manual](https://manual.audacityteam.org/man/label_tracks.html)
- Label Studio audio templates: Automatic Speech Recognition, ASR using Segments, Sound Event Detection, Audio Classification, Audio Classification with Segments (regions), Speaker Diarization, Voice/Signal detection, Contextual Scrolling — [Label Studio templates](https://labelstud.io/templates/); pre-annotations are imported as a `predictions` array (`model_version`, `score`, `result` regions with `from_name`/`to_name`/`type`) and "predictions cannot be modified and are always read-only", so annotators create corrected annotations which are then exported — [Label Studio predictions guide](https://labelstud.io/guide/predictions)
- CASD shows the per-beat chord-grid annotation paradigm Chordify uses internally (annotators pick a chord label per beat; difficulty and time recorded) — [chordify/CASD](https://github.com/chordify/CASD)
- Visual Guitar Tab Comparison aligns multiple tab versions bar-by-bar and highlights explicit differences, a reusable pattern for accept/reject-per-bar review — [arXiv 2311.14726](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.14726)
- OpenUkulele currently has no interactive editing ("outputs are final exports") — [xin-pu/OpenUkulele](https://github.com/xin-pu/OpenUkulele)

### Inferences
- A minimal correction loop for this pipeline: export per-stage results as Audacity label files (chords/beats/strums) and MIDI (notes) → fix in Audacity/Sonic Visualiser/Tony → re-import, which also yields corrected `.lab`/MIDI usable directly as mir_eval references and as training data. ## Key question 6: What accuracy makes a tab "usable"?

### Takeaway
No published threshold defines a "usable" automatic tab. The nearest evidence: guitarists rated human ground-truth tabs 7.45/10 and the best automatic fingering 6.04/10 for playability (MIDI-to-Tab user study); 2.4 % of chords with >7-fret stretches was called "a small but significant frequency" needing fixing; Chordify's annotator study shows expert chord annotators themselves disagree, and Moises openly states detection "does occasionally make mistakes", implying correction UIs are assumed.

### Cited Findings
- MIDI-to-Tab user study: 15 guitarists (median 10 years daily practice), 1–10 playability ratings while playing 4-bar excerpts; system 6.04±2.67 vs ground truth 7.45±2.62; "No explicit statement defined minimum acceptable accuracy"; 2.4 % of chords had stretches >7 frets, "a small but significant frequency" — [arXiv 2408.05024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.05024)
- Fretting-Transformer notes a trade-off between lower difficulty scores and higher tab accuracy across methods — [arXiv 2506.14223](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.14223)
- TabCNN: "The TDR of 0.899 indicates that over 89% of correctly identified pitches are assigned the correct fingering. This value is promising given that the majority of pitches playable on guitar can be played in multiple locations" — [TabCNN](https://zenodo.org/records/3527800)
- Murgul 2025 frames 92.75 % strum F1 on microphone audio as showing "strong potential for music education and DAW applications", with up-strums (~79 %) weaker than down-strums — [arXiv 2508.07973](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.07973)
- Lukoianov & Klapuri treat pattern discontinuity (15.4 % after smoothing vs 27.8 % before) as the readability criterion for rhythm-guitar transcriptions — [arXiv 2510.05756](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.05756)
- CASD documents that four expert annotators "disagree about chord labels" on the same 50 songs, at time-level and pitch-class level — [chordify/CASD](https://github.com/chordify/CASD)
- Moises: detection "is quite accurate, but it does occasionally make mistakes", hence manual editing — [Moises chord detection](https://moises.ai/features/chord-detection/)
- GOAT's authors judged their own tablature baseline as "results ultimately did not work well" at ~0.68 token accuracy / 0.52 WER — [arXiv 2509.22655](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.22655)

### Inferences
- A defensible internal target set, anchored to the literature rather than to a user-study threshold: note F_no_offset ≥ 0.8 (Basic Pitch on GuitarSet ≈0.79–0.84), tab TDR ≥ 0.9 (TabCNN 0.899), chord majmin in the 0.8s (MIREX-era systems), beat F-measure at 70 ms as reported by the beat tracker used, strum-direction F1 ≥ 0.85 down / ≥ 0.79 up (Murgul), and zero chords exceeding the Hand-Span Feasibility limit.
- Because even expert chord annotations disagree (CASD), chord accuracy should be reported against multiple references or with the `mirex` lenient vocabulary alongside `majmin`.

### Gaps
- Quantitative inter-annotator agreement figures from Koops et al. 2019 (JNMR) could not be retrieved (publisher page returned 403); only the qualitative statement from the CASD README is cited.
- No forum/user-study source stating a percentage threshold at which players consider an automatic tab "good enough" was found with the fetch-only method available.
- MIREX chord-estimation results for years with multiple submissions (e.g. 2019, 2021+) were not fetched; 2020 had a single system, so it does not establish a state-of-the-art ceiling.
