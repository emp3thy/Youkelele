# Review: rests and instrument activity (code map §6) against the published research

Written 2026-10-08 against main at 74c11e0. Read-only: the spike figures below come from four throwaway scripts run on the existing `runs\` folders (`spike_rests.py`, `spike_hpss.py`, `spike_edge.py`, `spike_pickup.py` in the session scratchpad). Ratios are the stage's `energy_ratio`; bar indices are grid indices.

## A. Diagnosis

**Common mechanism.** `bar_energy_ratio` (`src/youkelele/music/rests.py` line 29) takes one RMS over the exact `bar.start` to `bar.end`; `quantise_bar` and `bar_index` (`music/recall.py` line 79) assign onsets on a window shifted back by half a slot, so a stroke just before a downbeat belongs to the next bar. The two windows disagree on every bar's last half-slot, and the decision is a single-threshold mean with no memory (`bar_holds`, line 49: `REST_RATIO_MIN` 0.05 AND `REST_LOW_SHARE_MIN` 0.005), applied in `new_member` (`stages/strums.py` line 503) and `_bar_records` (line 344). The research names four gaps: bar-mean where the literature max-pools (Gururani 2018), one threshold where it uses an onset/offset pair with minimum durations (pyannote, Dinkel and Yu), a mix-relative floor where libraries normalise to the signal itself (MedleyDB, librosa `effects.split`), and energy alone where tail-versus-attack needs an ODF relative to its local average (madmom `pre_avg`).

**Chelsea Dagger 7 and 12 (0.050, 0.062; strokes nobody hears).** Eighth-slot RMS profiles: bar 7 `0 0 0 0 0 0.001 0 0.010`, bar 12 `0 0 0 0 0 0 0 0.016`. All the energy is the anticipated stab of bars 8 and 13 in the last eighth. On the half-slot-shifted window the ratios are 0.006 and 0.021: both rest. The cause is the window mismatch between `rests.py` and `recall.py`, not the floor.

**Summer of '69 pickup bar 0 (0.024; heard "from the start").** Reclassified. Bar 0 is the grid's one-beat pickup, 0.02 to 0.44 s. The stem is digital zero until 0.38 s; its first attack is at 0.42 to 0.44 s, the downbeat of bar 1; the slot profile is `0.0001 ... 0.0096`, everything in the last eighth. The ear is right that the guitar starts with the song's first audible moment, but no guitar is inside bar 0 (shifted window: 0.000). Max-pooling cannot help: a one-beat bar's beat-max is its mean. This is a grid and page problem (a one-beat anacrusis printed as an empty full bar, read as a missed pickup), see B5. Clip 7's verdict should be re-read against this timing before A4 changes.

**Carried tails (Pour Some Sugar On Me 7 to 10 with 8, 6, 4, 7 onsets; Wet Leg 18 with 6; Pour Some Sugar On Me 77 with 3; all rest, all ear-confirmed).** Right only because their ratios (0.0017 to 0.0499) sit under the floor. The onset detector fires on them because librosa's picker runs on a globally normalised envelope and the stem's tail and musical-noise artefacts produce flux in near-silence (bar 9 at ratio 0.002 carries four onsets). Any fix that lowers the floor, adds energy hysteresis or gates on onset count un-rests them. Their slot profiles show the tail signature: energy in the first slots, then decay with no re-rise (bar 7: `0.0047 0.0065 0.0016 0.0003 0 0 0 0`). HPSS percussive-over-harmonic does not separate them (bars 7 and 8 at −9 and −11 dB, bar 77 at +8 dB; holding bars span −5 to +5 dB).

**The vote moved; two intros flipped to certain-and-wrong.** `_vote_member` (line 216) reads `classes[b] for b in member.holding`; confidence, `structure_test` and the `UNCERTAIN_BELOW` 0.45 floor (`music/as_played.py` line 18) are computed on holding bars alone. Chelsea Dagger's intro went from 21 bars to 11 at confidence 0.49, p 0.001; Need You Tonight 0 to 13 to 5 bars at 0.75. Discarding low-evidence bars raised confidence across the floor, and spec 4.5 has no length term. The research's rule (decide with hysteresis and a minimum duration) applies to certainty as much as to activity.

**Three references.** `section_has_instrument` (`music/onsets.py` line 87, `SECTION_MIN_RATIO` 0.10) is the mix-relative mean at section scale, unrelated to the bar floor; `silent_bar_mask` (`recall.py` line 64, `SILENT_BAR_SHARE` 0.25 of the median bar RMS) is a third, song-relative rule for the recall gate only.

## B. Suggestions, ranked

**B1. Measure activity on the quantiser's window.** `rests.py` `bar_energy_ratio` and `bar_low_share`: slice `[bar.start − half_slot, bar.end − half_slot)` with `half_slot = (bar.end − bar.start) / slots_per_bar / 2`, the shift `recall.bar_index` uses; pass `slots_per_bar` from the stage (known at line 493). Fixes Chelsea 7 and 12 and makes the pickup bar consistent with where its note prints (bar 1, slot 0). Research: Gururani's point that a window should own the attacks inside it ([ISMIR 2018](https://ismir2018.ismir.net/doc/pdfs/275_Paper.pdf)); the half-slot convention is how this pipeline already defines "inside". Risk to the 46 confirmed rests: Need You Tonight 7 and Wet Leg 25 (anticipation-only bars) fall to 0.000; Pour Some Sugar On Me 77 rises from 0.029 to 0.068 because the shift pulls in bar 76's last half-slot, so it holds unless B3 rests it. Generality: a convention, no constant. Install: none.

**B2. A song-relative reference in dB, beside the mix ratio.** `rests.py`: per bar, `rel_db = 20 log10(max 100 ms frame RMS in the shifted window / p90 of bar maxima over the song)`; keep `REST_RATIO_MIN` (it says "too quiet to matter in the mix", which no song-relative figure gives) and add `REST_REL_DB_MIN` as a second condition. Research: MedleyDB normalises to the summed-stem peak envelope, librosa `effects.split` to the signal's max ([activation_conf.py](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/marl/medleydb/master/medleydb/annotate/activation_conf.py), [librosa effects.py](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/librosa/librosa/main/librosa/effects.py)). Measured on five songs: holding bars' 5th percentile of frame-max re p90 is −7 to −14 dB; confirmed tails −8 to −39 dB; the bands touch at −8 dB (Wet Leg 18 at −8.1 resting, Chelsea 9 at −8.9 holding). So B2 cannot replace the ratio floor; its value is removing the loud-chorus bias the research names. Do not use "dB above the stem's bleed floor": the Demucs stem is digital zero where it finds no guitar (10th-percentile frame RMS 0.00003 to 0.0003 on four of five songs), so every bar reads +40 to +70 dB above it. Risk: no constant exists until the D3 sweep. Install: none.

**B3. A re-rise test to tell a tail from a stroke.** New `bar_has_attack(stem_22k, bar, slots)` in `rests.py`: per-slot RMS on the shifted window; an attack is a slot after the first rising at least `ATTACK_RISE_DB` above the minimum of the preceding slots, or slot 0 rising that much above the previous bar's last slot. A bar over the energy floors with no attack is a tail: (a) in `bar_holds` it rests (fixes Pour Some Sugar On Me 77 after B1; rests 7 to 10 and Wet Leg 18 on evidence rather than the floor's luck); (b) in `_bar_records` it carries a `tail` flag the renderer may later draw as a tie or "let ring". Research: madmom's `pre_avg` and SuperFlux's ratio to the local novelty average ([madmom onsets.py](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/CPJKU/madmom/main/madmom/features/onsets.py), [SuperFluxPeaks](https://essentia.upf.edu/reference/std_SuperFluxPeaks.html)). Prefer slot-RMS rise to HPSS: perc/harm overlaps (A) and HPSS costs a full-song STFT. Risk: a whole-note strum on slot 0 must count its rise against the previous bar's last slot; test with a synthetic ringing chord per bar. Generality: a dB rise, loudness invariant. Install: none.

**B4. Hysteresis and minimum durations in beats, after B1 to B3.** A state machine in `rests.py` (`activity_states(energy, rel_db, attack, beats_per_bar)`): enter rest below `REST_ENTER`, leave only above `REST_LEAVE` with an attack; `MIN_OFF_BEATS` fills a lone sub-bar gap inside a played phrase; `MIN_ON_BEATS` drops a lone holding bar inside a rest unless it has an attack. Research: pyannote's onset/offset pairs with independent `min_duration_on` and `min_duration_off` ([pyannote/segmentation](https://huggingface.co/pyannote/segmentation)); Dinkel and Yu's double threshold ([arXiv 1904.03841](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/1904.03841)). Fourth because the data show no flicker yet: the two near-floor bars were boundary leakage, and energy-only hysteresis un-rests Pour Some Sugar On Me 7 (0.0499 after a holding bar) unless B3 gates it. Risk: three constants with no band until D3. Install: none.

**B5. The pickup bar is an anacrusis, not a rest.** Six of eleven grids have a bar 0 shorter than the meter (one to three beats). In `new_member` or the score builder, mark a bar shorter than the meter at index 0 as `pickup`; the renderer prints a partial bar rather than an empty full bar; it holds or rests by the ordinary rule. Research: Finale's "minimize number of rests" ([Finale quantization](https://usermanuals.finalemusic.com/FinaleWin/Content/Finale/QUANTMORESETTINGS.htm)). Risk: none to the 46 rests; check the grid stage's definition of bar 0 first (D4).

**B6. Certainty must not flip because the sample shrank.** In `_vote_member`, keep the vote and figures on `member.holding`, and also compute confidence and `structure_test` on the full analysed span (the 1.6 reading); set `uncertain` when either reading is uncertain, and record both (`confidence_all_bars`, `chance_p_all_bars` on `SectionPattern`). The pattern stays the holding-bar vote: resting bars carry no rhythm, and Wet Leg 26 to 32 became right that way. Research: the double-threshold principle (a confident label needs the stronger evidence). Addresses the Chelsea Dagger and Need You Tonight flips; Pour Some Sugar On Me 67 to 77 is a riff-flag matter. Risk: only a member 1.6 greyed for noise and 1.7 rightly blackened could grey again; D5 lists them. Generality: no constant. Install: none.

**B7. One activity reference.** Derive the section cut from the bar rule (a section holds when at least `MIN_ON_BEATS` of its bars hold) and let `silent_bar_mask` read the bar `rests` flags. Measure on D1 first.

## E. Do not do

- Do not lower `REST_RATIO_MIN` to catch the pickup bar: the stem is silent inside it, and every tail between 0.024 and 0.05 would hold.
- Do not gate activity on onset count: near-silent stems carry 3 to 8 onsets per bar from normalised-envelope flux.
- Do not max-pool over the exact bar window: it un-rests anticipation-only bars the ear confirmed silent.
- Do not express the threshold as dB above the stem's bleed floor: Demucs outputs digital zero, not bleed.
- Do not use HPSS perc/harm as the tail test on its own.
- Do not add a pretrained activity detector (Essentia TF models, OpenMIC): 1 s resolution, Linux-only wheels, guitar the weakest class.
- Do not fit `REST_REL_DB_MIN` or the hysteresis pair without leave-one-song-out: the labelled rests sit in two songs.
- Do not restore the full-span vote to undo the changed rows; Wet Leg 26 to 32 became right because resting bars left it. Change the certainty rule (B6), not the vote.
- Do not change A4 on clip 7 until the pickup timing in A is re-heard with the bar line marked.
